Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Will servicing my car at Neale Goad void my new-car warranty?

No. Under Australian Consumer Law, you are free to have your vehicle serviced by any qualified workshop — including an independent Repco Authorised Service Centre like ours. Provided the service follows the manufacturer's logbook schedule and uses parts that meet specification, your warranty remains fully intact. We use genuine and OEM-equivalent parts and stamp your logbook to schedule.

Do you service all makes and models?

Yes. Our factory-trained technicians work on all makes and models — European, Japanese, Korean, American and Australian vehicles. From small city cars to large SUVs and utes. There's no brand we won't work on.

How often should I have my car serviced?

Most manufacturers recommend servicing every 6 months or 10,000–15,000 km, whichever comes first. Check your logbook for the exact schedule for your vehicle. If you're unsure, give us a call and we can advise based on your make, model and driving conditions.

What's included in a basic car service?

Our essential service includes engine oil and filter replacement, fluid level checks and top-ups, tyre pressure and condition check, battery and charging test, and a multi-point safety inspection covering brakes, lights, belts and hoses.

How long does a car service take?

Most services are completed within a few hours. We're usually able to service and return your vehicle the same day. With our free pickup and delivery service (within 10 km of the CBD), many customers don't need to take time off work at all.

What is the free pickup and delivery service?

We offer free pickup and delivery of your vehicle within a 10 km radius of the Ballarat CBD. Simply book your service, leave your car at home or work, and we'll collect it, complete the work, and return it to you — all without disrupting your day.
