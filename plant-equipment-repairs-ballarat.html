Hydraulic Systems

Full Hydraulic Capability — Up to 350 Bar

Hydraulic failure is the most common cause of plant downtime. Our workshop carries the equipment and skill to diagnose, repair and test hydraulic systems to OEM specification — getting your machine back in service with confidence.

  • Pressure testing to 350 bar — confirm full system integrity before the machine leaves
  • Hydraulic cylinder and ram repair — rebore, seal replacement and rod repair or replacement
  • Pump and motor rebuilds — gear, piston and vane type hydraulic pumps
  • Control valve service — directional, relief and proportional valves cleaned, inspected and set
  • Hydraulic hose and fitting fabrication — custom lines made in-house for fast turnaround
  • Hydraulic oil sampling and analysis — identify contamination and wear metals before they cause major damage
Hydraulic Cylinder / Ram Repair — Excavator Component
Workshop-based repair is faster and more thorough. When plant can be transported to our Wendouree facility, we can perform full hydraulic circuit testing, engine diagnostics and mechanical repair under one roof — rather than limiting diagnosis to what's accessible in the field.

Fault Diagnosis

Systematic Diagnosis — Not Guesswork

Modern plant operates complex hydraulic, electronic and mechanical systems simultaneously. We use a disciplined diagnostic method to correctly identify root cause — so we're repairing the actual problem, not chasing symptoms.

Electronic Fault Code Reading
All active and historical fault codes retrieved. We interpret codes in context — a fault code is a starting point, not an automatic parts order.
Live Data Monitoring
Engine load, hydraulic pressures, temperatures and sensor outputs monitored in real time during operation — revealing intermittent faults that static testing misses.
Hydraulic Pressure Testing
System pressure, relief valve settings, pump output and cylinder leakage tested against OEM specifications — isolating whether the problem is pump, valve or actuator.

Common Questions

Plant & Equipment Repairs FAQ

What types of plant equipment do you work on?

We service and repair excavators, skid steer loaders, wheel loaders, backhoes, motor graders, compactors, telehandlers, forklifts and general construction plant. If it moves on a job site and needs a mechanic, we have the capability.

Do you do hydraulic repairs on-site at the workshop?

Yes. All hydraulic diagnosis and repair work is carried out in our Wendouree workshop. We pressure-test hydraulic systems up to 350 bar, repair or replace hydraulic cylinders and rams, rebuild pumps and motors, replace hoses and fittings, and fabricate custom hydraulic lines. For equipment that can be transported, workshop repair is generally more efficient than field service.

Can you rebuild the engine in a piece of plant?

Yes. We carry out in-frame and full engine rebuilds for diesel-powered plant including common-rail and mechanically-injected engines. We manage all machining liaison (cylinder head reconditioning, line boring, etc.) and rebuild to OEM specification.

What happens if our machine breaks down mid-project?

Call us on (03) 5339 2056 as soon as possible. We'll discuss the symptoms and, where possible, advise on whether the machine can safely continue operating or needs to come off. For urgent situations, we prioritise diagnosis and turnaround to get your project back on schedule.

Do you carry out scheduled plant maintenance?

Yes — preventative maintenance is far more cost-effective than emergency repairs. We set up maintenance schedules for plant operators and construction companies, tracking service intervals by hours or calendar time, and providing service records for each piece of equipment.

What brands of plant equipment do you service?

We work across all major brands including Caterpillar, Komatsu, Hitachi, Volvo, Kubota, Bobcat, JCB, Case and Doosan. Our diagnostic equipment covers multiplex CAN-bus systems on modern plant as well as older mechanical equipment.
