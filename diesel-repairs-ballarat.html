Compliance & Certification

Heavy Vehicle
Roadworthy & NHVR

As a Licensed Vehicle Tester, we carry full accreditation to issue heavy vehicle roadworthy certificates and conduct NHVR compliance inspections. With two inspectors on-site and calibrated roller brake testing equipment, we can complete the full compliance assessment in a single visit.

  • NHVR Framework Inspections — accredited assessments under the National Heavy Vehicle Regulator
  • Roller Brake Testing — calibrated onsite equipment meeting roadside enforcement standards
  • Heavy Vehicle Roadworthy Certificates — rigid trucks, articulated vehicles, trailers and buses
  • Pre-Enforcement Inspections — identify and resolve compliance issues before roadside intercept
  • Trusted by Government & Health — Victoria SES, Grampians Health and Federation University fleet inspections

Operators subject to random roadside interception should consider a pre-enforcement inspection. We identify brake, lighting, load-restraint and structural defects before they become infringement notices or vehicles placed Out of Service.

Frequently Asked Questions

Diesel Repairs
— Common Questions

What diesel vehicles do you work on?

We service and repair all diesel-powered vehicles and equipment including light diesel cars and utes, trucks (rigid and articulated), prime movers, buses, excavators, loaders, backhoes, graders and general plant. If it runs on diesel, we have the equipment and expertise to work on it.

What diagnostic equipment do you use?

We use dealer-level diagnostic tooling capable of multiplex and CAN-bus scanning across European, Japanese and American heavy vehicles. This allows us to diagnose fault codes, test injector performance, check rail pressure and monitor live sensor data — the same depth of analysis available at a manufacturer's workshop, without the dealer price.

Can you service DPF systems?

Yes. We service and repair Diesel Particulate Filter (DPF) systems including forced regeneration, cleaning, fault diagnosis and where necessary, replacement. DPF issues left unaddressed can cause injector damage and engine failure — early diagnosis saves significant cost.

Do you carry out heavy vehicle compliance inspections?

Yes. As a Licensed Vehicle Tester we are accredited to conduct heavy vehicle roadworthy inspections and compliance assessments under the NHVR framework. We also have calibrated onsite roller brake testing equipment for brake performance compliance — important for operators subject to roadside enforcement.

Can you repair hydraulic systems?

Yes — hydraulic diagnosis and repair is a specialist capability. We pressure-test hydraulic systems up to 350 bar, rebuild cylinders and rams, replace seals and hoses, and fabricate custom hydraulic lines. We work on excavators, loaders, tippers, cranes and other hydraulic plant.

How do I book a diesel inspection or repair?

Call (03) 5339 2056 to discuss your vehicle and the symptoms you're experiencing. For large vehicles or plant equipment, we'll arrange a suitable time — early bookings are recommended as workshop capacity for heavy vehicles is limited.
