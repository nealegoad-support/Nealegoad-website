Common questions

Air Conditioning FAQ

How do I know if my car's A/C needs a re-gas?

The most obvious sign is that the air conditioning isn't as cold as it used to be, or not cold at all. Other signs include the system taking a long time to cool the cabin, or the compressor cycling on and off rapidly. If in doubt, call us.

What is involved in an A/C re-gas?

We recover any remaining refrigerant, check the system for leaks, replace the refrigerant to the correct specification, and test system performance. On many vehicles we also replace the receiver/drier as standard practice.

What refrigerant does my car use?

Most vehicles made before about 2015 use R134a. Newer vehicles typically use R1234yf, which has lower environmental impact. We have equipment for both types — you don't need to know which your car uses.

How often should I service my car's A/C?

Most manufacturers recommend A/C servicing every 2–3 years. We recommend an annual A/C check as part of your regular service, particularly given Ballarat's summer conditions.

Can a bad smell from the A/C be fixed?

Yes. Musty or foul smells are almost always caused by bacterial growth on the evaporator. We use professional evaporator treatment and replace the cabin air filter — the smell should be gone after treatment.

What's the difference between a re-gas and a full A/C service?

A re-gas tops up the refrigerant. A full service goes further — pressure and performance testing, compressor and condenser inspection, cabin filter replacement, and a re-gas. If the system hasn't been serviced in several years, a full service is the better investment.
