Common questions

Mechanical Repairs FAQ

What types of mechanical repairs do you carry out?

We carry out the full range of mechanical repairs: brakes (pads, discs, calipers, lines), suspension (springs, shock absorbers, bushes, ball joints), steering (rack, power steering, alignment), engine (timing belts/chains, head gaskets, oil seals, injectors), cooling system (radiator, thermostat, water pump), exhaust, transmission, driveline and electrical/electronic diagnosis.

Do you use genuine parts or aftermarket?

We use the best parts for the job. For most repairs, Repco-supplied parts (meeting or exceeding OEM specifications) deliver excellent quality at fair cost. For some makes or specific components, we source genuine manufacturer parts. We discuss options with you before proceeding.

What warranty do I get on repairs?

All repairs are backed by the Repco Authorised Service nationwide warranty — valid at any Repco Authorised Service Centre across Australia. The warranty covers parts and labour on covered repairs.

My warning light is on but the car seems to drive fine. Should I get it checked?

Yes. Some warning lights indicate urgent issues (oil pressure, brake system); others indicate sensor or emissions issues that won't affect driving immediately but will worsen. We use professional diagnostic scanning equipment to read and interpret fault codes.

How long will my repair take?

Many common repairs — brakes, suspension components, belts — can be completed same-day or next-day. Complex jobs may take longer. We provide a realistic timeframe when we assess the vehicle and confirm before proceeding.

Do I need to book for mechanical repairs?

Yes — we recommend booking so we can allocate the right bay and technician. Call (03) 5339 2056. For urgent safety concerns like brakes or steering, let us know and we'll prioritise accordingly.
