Fuel Cost Savings

Why LPG Makes
Financial Sense

LPG has consistently traded at a substantial discount to unleaded petrol at the bowser. For vehicles that cover significant distances — particularly utes, SUVs, taxis, couriers and commercial fleets — the savings per kilometre add up quickly and conversion costs are often recovered within the first year or two of driving.

  • Lower cost per litre — LPG has typically been 40–60% cheaper than ULP per litre at the bowser.
  • High-mileage benefits — commercial vehicles and high-km drivers typically recover conversion costs fastest.
  • Dual-fuel operation — retain petrol as a backup with a dual-fuel system for maximum flexibility.
  • Payback estimate — we can model a payback calculation specific to your vehicle and annual kilometres.

Ask us for a payback calculation. When you enquire, tell us your annual kilometres and we can give you a realistic estimate of how long your LPG conversion will take to pay for itself at current LPG prices.

Standards & Compliance

Certified to
Australian Standards

Every LPG conversion completed at Neale Goad Automotive is installed to Australian Standard AS/NZS 1425 — the mandatory standard governing LPG fuel systems in motor vehicles. You receive full compliance documentation on completion.

01

AS/NZS 1425 Compliance

All installations meet the mandatory Australian Standard for LPG fuel systems in motor vehicles, covering tank placement, fittings, hose routing and safety device requirements.

02

Compliance Certificate

You receive a signed compliance certificate with every conversion. This documentation is required for registration purposes and confirms your LPG system meets current regulations.

03

Roadworthy Compatible

A properly certified LPG conversion does not affect your ability to obtain a roadworthy certificate. As a Licensed Vehicle Tester (LVT), we can inspect and certify LPG-converted vehicles on-site.

What's Involved

LPG System
Components

A complete LPG conversion involves a number of precision-fitted components, all installed by our experienced technicians to meet Australian Standards. Here's what goes into a quality LPG system.

01

LPG Cylinder / Tank

Certified multi-valve cylinder mounted in the boot or under the vehicle, sized to balance range with available storage space. All tanks are tested and certified to Australian Standards.

02

Reducer / Vaporiser

Converts liquid LPG from the tank to a vapour at the correct pressure for engine delivery, integrated with the coolant system to maintain optimal operating temperature.

03

LPG Injector Rail

Sequential injection systems use individual injectors for each cylinder, providing precise fuel delivery for optimal performance and economy — closely matching petrol injection calibration.

04

ECU & Switch

A dedicated LPG electronic control unit manages fuel delivery and transitions between LPG and petrol. A dash-mounted switch lets the driver select fuel or switch to petrol at any time.

Common Questions

LPG Conversions
FAQs

How much can I save with LPG?

LPG typically costs significantly less per litre than petrol, and while an LPG engine uses slightly more gas by volume than petrol, the overall fuel cost per kilometre is generally lower. The exact savings depend on your vehicle, driving patterns and current fuel prices. For high-mileage vehicles — particularly utes, SUVs and commercial vehicles — the savings can be substantial over the life of the vehicle. We can provide an indicative payback calculation for your specific vehicle when you enquire.

Is my car eligible for LPG conversion?

Most petrol-powered vehicles can be converted to LPG or dual-fuel (petrol and LPG) operation. Eligibility depends on the engine type, age and configuration. Modern direct-injection engines require specific LPG system types. We assess your vehicle before providing a quote to confirm suitability and recommend the right system for your application.

Will an LPG conversion affect my roadworthy certificate?

No — provided the LPG system is installed to Australian Standards (AS/NZS 1425) and certified correctly, an LPG conversion does not affect your ability to obtain a roadworthy certificate. In fact, as a Licensed Vehicle Tester, we can inspect and certify LPG-converted vehicles as part of our normal roadworthy service. We provide all compliance documentation with every conversion we complete.

Does LPG conversion affect engine performance?

A well-installed LPG system should have minimal impact on engine performance. Some conversion types result in a slight reduction in power output compared to petrol, particularly under heavy load, but for most daily driving conditions the difference is barely noticeable. Modern sequential injection LPG systems minimise this effect by delivering fuel with high precision matched closely to the vehicle's original tuning.

How often does an LPG system need servicing?

LPG systems generally require a service inspection every 12 months or 20,000 km (whichever comes first), in addition to your normal vehicle service. This includes inspecting the tank, valves, fittings, hoses and fuel injection components for wear and compliance. We incorporate LPG system checks into our standard service schedule for converted vehicles so you don't need to track it separately.

Can you service and repair existing LPG systems?

Yes. If your vehicle already has an LPG system installed — regardless of where the original conversion was done — we can inspect, service and repair it as part of your regular vehicle maintenance. We also carry out compliance re-certifications where required for registration or inspection purposes. Call us to discuss your existing system.
