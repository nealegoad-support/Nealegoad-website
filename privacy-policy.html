2. Information we collect

We may collect the following personal information:

  • Contact details: name, phone number, email address
  • Vehicle information: make, model, year, registration number
  • Service history: details of work carried out at our workshop
  • Communication records: emails, phone notes, booking requests
  • Website usage data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, time on site (via Google Analytics)

We collect this information directly from you when you call, email, fill in a form, or visit our website. We do not collect sensitive information (as defined in the Privacy Act) unless required for a specific service.

3. Why we collect your information

We use your personal information to:

  • Book and manage vehicle service appointments
  • Provide quotations, repair authorisation and invoicing
  • Contact you regarding your vehicle and service history
  • Process warranty claims through Repco Authorised Service
  • Improve our services and website
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

4. Google Ads and remarketing

Our website uses Google Ads and Google Analytics (including Google Tag Manager). These services may:

  • Set cookies to track your visit and measure conversions (e.g. phone clicks, form submissions)
  • Show you our ads on Google Search, YouTube and the Google Display Network after you have visited our site (remarketing)
  • Use aggregated data about your visits to improve ad targeting — no individually identifying data is shared with Google for this purpose

You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time via Google's ad settings or by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

5. Cookies

Our website uses cookies — small text files stored on your device. We use:

  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics): to understand how visitors use the site
  • Advertising cookies (Google Ads): to measure conversions and serve relevant ads

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some functionality of this website.

6. Disclosure of your information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Repco Authorised Service: for warranty processing and parts sourcing
  • Google LLC: for analytics and advertising services (governed by Google's own privacy policy)
  • Legal authorities: when required by law or to protect our legal rights

7. Storage and security

Your information is stored securely within Australia and in third-party cloud services subject to equivalent protections. We take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss, unauthorised access, modification or disclosure.

8. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 1988, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Complain about a breach of the Australian Privacy Principles

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Complaints

If you are dissatisfied with how we handle your personal information, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at www.oaic.gov.au or by calling 1300 363 992.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version is always available at this URL. Material changes will be noted with an updated effective date.
