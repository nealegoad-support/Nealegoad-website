Common questions

EV Service FAQ

Can you service electric and hybrid vehicles?

Yes. We service and repair EV and hybrid vehicles using equipment and procedures appropriate to high-voltage systems. Our technicians follow manufacturer safety protocols for all EV work.

What EV services do you offer?

We offer battery health checks, charging system diagnosis and repair, thermal management servicing, brake system service (including regenerative braking), air conditioning, general vehicle logbook servicing and fault diagnosis on EV and hybrid models.

Is my EV warranty safe if I service with you?

Under Australian Consumer Law, you may have your vehicle serviced by any qualified workshop without voiding your manufacturer warranty, provided the service follows the manufacturer's schedule and uses appropriate parts. We advise on requirements for each specific vehicle.

What EV brands do you work on?

We work on all major EV and hybrid brands available in Australia including Toyota, Hyundai, Kia, BYD, MG, GWM, Mitsubishi, Nissan, Volvo and more. Call us to discuss your specific vehicle.

My EV has a warning light — what should I do?

Call us to describe the warning. Some EV warning lights require immediate attention (battery or charging faults); others are routine service reminders. We'll advise on urgency and arrange diagnosis promptly. For battery or power system warnings, don't delay — call us first.
