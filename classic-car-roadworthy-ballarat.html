Common questions

Classic Car Roadworthy FAQ

What is the VicRoads Club Permit Scheme (CPS)?

The CPS is a VicRoads program that allows eligible historic vehicles to be operated on the road under a permit system rather than standard registration. Vehicles must be 25 or more years old, the owner must hold membership of a VicRoads-recognised car club, and the vehicle must pass a safety inspection by a Licensed Vehicle Tester.

Is my classic vehicle eligible for a club permit?

Generally yes, if it's 25+ years old, you're a member of a VicRoads-recognised historic vehicle club, and the vehicle is in a safe, roadworthy condition. Call us to discuss your specific vehicle.

What does a classic vehicle roadworthy inspection involve?

The same safety areas as a standard roadworthy — brakes, tyres, steering, suspension, lights, body, glass and seatbelts — but applied to the context of a historic vehicle. We understand that classic vehicles have age-appropriate characteristics and inspect accordingly.

How often does a classic vehicle need a CPS inspection?

Club permit vehicles generally require an annual safety inspection to renew the permit. We can advise on your specific requirements.

Can you service and maintain my classic as well as inspect it?

Yes. We offer mechanical servicing and sympathetic repairs for classic vehicles — from routine maintenance to brake rebuilding and fault diagnosis.

Do you have experience with specific classic vehicle makes?

We have experience with a wide range of Australian and imported classics — Holden, Ford, Chrysler, Valiant, British and European classics, American muscle and vintage motorcycles. Call us to discuss your specific machine.
