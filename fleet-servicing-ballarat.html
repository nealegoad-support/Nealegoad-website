Common questions

Fleet Servicing FAQ

What size fleets do you service?

We work with fleets from a handful of vehicles up to large institutional operations. Our current partners include Victoria SES, Grampians Health, Federation University and Ballarat Hospice Care. Whether you run 5 vehicles or 50+, we'll tailor a maintenance schedule and reporting format that works for your operation.

Can you handle diesel and heavy vehicles in our fleet?

Yes — heavy vehicle and diesel capability is a core strength. We have specialist technicians, dealer-level diagnostic equipment, and calibrated onsite roller brake testing for compliance.

What does single-invoice reporting mean?

Rather than separate invoices per vehicle, we consolidate all fleet work into a single monthly invoice with a full breakdown by vehicle. This simplifies your accounts and gives your fleet manager clear cost oversight.

Do you offer priority booking for fleet customers?

Yes. Fleet partners receive priority booking status, meaning your vehicles are scheduled ahead of general bookings. A vehicle off the road is a service interruption — we treat it that way.

Can you handle compliance inspections and roadworthies?

Yes. As a Licensed Vehicle Tester we complete roadworthy certificates, heavy vehicle inspections and compliance documentation as part of fleet contracts, removing the need for a separate inspection provider.

How do I get a fleet proposal?

Complete the enquiry form on this page or call (03) 5339 2056 and ask to speak to our fleet specialist. We'll discuss your vehicle mix and requirements, then provide a tailored proposal within one business day.
