The Inspection

What We Check —
All 8 VicRoads Areas

Every roadworthy inspection at Neale Goad Automotive covers all areas mandated by VicRoads. Our inspectors don't skip points or rush the process — your certificate means something.

Inspection Area What We Look For Standard
Brakes Pad and shoe thickness, rotor condition, brake fluid, lines and hoses, handbrake function, pedal travel and response under load VicRoads 7.1
Steering & Suspension Steering play and response, rack and pinion condition, ball joints, tie rod ends, control arms, wheel bearings, strut/shock condition and ride height VicRoads 7.2
Tyres & Wheels Minimum 1.5 mm tread depth across full width, sidewall integrity, tyre age and cracking, matching sizes on axles, wheel nut torque and condition VicRoads 7.3
Lights & Electrical Headlights (alignment & output), indicators, hazard lights, brake lights, reverse lights, number plate lighting, interior warning lights and horn VicRoads 7.4
Body & Chassis Structural integrity of chassis rails, floor pan and firewall, door hinges and latches, bonnet and boot latching, corrosion assessment in load-bearing areas VicRoads 7.5
Windscreen & Glass Windscreen cracks and chips in driver sightlines, wiper blade condition and sweep, washer function, tint legality on front glass VicRoads 7.6
Seatbelts & Restraints Belt webbing condition (cuts, fraying, fading), buckle latch and release, retractor function, anchorage points and child seat attachment points VicRoads 7.7
Exhaust & Driveline Exhaust system integrity (leaks, noise, fume intrusion into cabin), CV joint and driveshaft condition, diff and gearbox mounts, fuel system for leaks VicRoads 7.8

All inspections follow the current VicRoads Roadworthiness Manual to the letter. Our inspectors are audited annually and our workshop holds an active, clean testing licence. When you receive a certificate from Neale Goad Automotive, it is unimpeachable.

Frequently Asked

Roadworthy
Questions Answered

How much does a roadworthy certificate cost in Ballarat?

Roadworthy certificate pricing depends on your vehicle type. Light vehicles typically start from a competitive flat rate — call us on (03) 5339 2056 for a current price on your specific vehicle. Because we're a full workshop, if minor faults are found we can often quote and complete rectification the same day, saving you a second trip.

How long does a roadworthy inspection take?

Most light vehicle inspections take between 1 and 1.5 hours. Motorcycles are typically quicker. Trucks and heavy vehicles require more time. We offer same-week availability and will give you an accurate timeframe when you book.

What happens if my vehicle fails the inspection?

You receive a written report detailing exactly what didn't pass. Because Neale Goad Automotive is a full-service workshop with two accredited inspectors, we can complete rectification repairs on-site — so you don't need a second trip to a different workshop. Once repairs are done, we re-inspect and issue the certificate.

How long is a roadworthy certificate valid for?

In Victoria, a Certificate of Roadworthiness is generally valid for 30 days from the date of issue for the purpose of transferring registration or selling a vehicle. If you're registering a previously unregistered vehicle, different rules may apply — we'll guide you through what applies to your situation.

Do I need a roadworthy certificate to sell my car?

In Victoria, you generally need a current roadworthy certificate to transfer registration when selling a registered vehicle privately. There are some exemptions (e.g., selling to a licensed dealer). Our inspectors can advise on what applies to your specific situation.

What vehicles can you inspect?

We are accredited to inspect cars, motorcycles, light commercials, trucks, heavy vehicles, and trailers. We are also accredited for Club Permit Scheme (CPS) safety inspections on classic and vintage vehicles 25 years and older.

Can I book a same-day roadworthy?

We regularly accommodate same-week bookings, and often same-day if you call first thing in the morning. Call (03) 5339 2056 to check current availability.
