Common questions

Logbook Servicing FAQ

Will logbook servicing at Neale Goad void my new-car warranty?

No. Under Australian Consumer Law, you are free to have your vehicle serviced by any qualified workshop — including an independent Repco Authorised Service Centre like ours. Provided the service follows the manufacturer's logbook schedule and uses parts that meet specification, your warranty remains fully intact. We stamp your logbook and provide written records of all work completed.

Do you use genuine parts?

We use genuine manufacturer parts or OEM-equivalent parts that meet or exceed the manufacturer's specifications. We'll always advise which parts we're using. For most scheduled logbook services, quality OEM-equivalent parts deliver identical performance and are fully warranty-compliant.

Do you stamp my logbook after the service?

Yes — every time. We stamp and date your logbook at every scheduled service. This maintains a complete, official service history which protects your warranty and preserves your vehicle's resale value.

How do I know when my logbook service is due?

Your due date is recorded from the previous service in your logbook. Most vehicles also show a dashboard service reminder. If you're unsure, call us with your make, model and year and we'll check the manufacturer's recommended interval.

Do you service all car brands?

Yes — European, Japanese, Korean, American and Australian vehicles. From Toyota and Mazda to BMW, Mercedes, Audi and Volkswagen. If it has a logbook, we can service it to schedule.

What's the difference between logbook and standard servicing?

Logbook servicing follows the manufacturer's specific schedule — particular fluids, filters, inspections and items that must be replaced at each interval. For new or near-new vehicles still under warranty, logbook servicing is essential.
