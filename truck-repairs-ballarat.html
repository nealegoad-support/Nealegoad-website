Brake Compliance

Brake Testing

One of very few independent workshops in Ballarat with calibrated roller brake testing equipment built into the floor. Your truck gets tested and certified without needing to travel to a separate facility.

Axle-by-Axle Brake Force Measurement
Individual assessment of brake force and imbalance per axle — identifies weak or seized brakes before they become a defect notice.
NHVR-Compliant Certification
Test reports printed and retained for compliance records. Results accepted for NHVR Annual Inspection and operator accreditation programs.
Same-Day Rectification

Full inspection covering steering, brakes, lighting, tyres, glazing and structural integrity. CoR issued on-site by our accredited inspectors — not outsourced.

Common Questions

Truck Repair
FAQs

Questions about bringing a large vehicle to our workshop, compliance requirements or what we can handle. If your question isn't covered below, call us directly.

(03) 5339 2056
What types of trucks do you service?

We service and repair rigid trucks, prime movers (including B-doubles and road trains), tippers, curtainsiders, flat tops, refrigerated trucks, buses and other heavy commercial vehicles. We work across all major brands including Kenworth, Mack, Volvo, Mercedes, Isuzu, Hino and Mitsubishi Fuso.

Do you carry out heavy vehicle roadworthy inspections?

Yes. As a Licensed Vehicle Tester accredited for heavy vehicles, we conduct roadworthy inspections for all classes of commercial trucks and heavy vehicles. We issue Certificates of Roadworthiness and can rectify identified defects on-site to avoid multiple trips.

What is roller brake testing and do you offer it?

Roller brake testing assesses the braking performance of each axle individually, measuring brake force and imbalance. It's a key compliance requirement for heavy vehicles. We have calibrated onsite roller brake testing equipment, meaning your truck can be tested and certified without travelling to a separate facility.

Can you rebuild heavy vehicle engines?

Yes. We carry out in-frame and full engine rebuilds for diesel engines including common-rail systems. We liaise with specialist machine shops for cylinder head reconditioning and precision work, and reassemble to OEM specification.

How do I bring a large truck in for service?

Call ahead on (03) 5339 2056 to arrange a suitable bay and time. Our workshop in Wendouree is equipped for large commercial vehicles. For urgent breakdowns or if the vehicle can't be driven, we can advise on towing to our workshop.

Do you service truck hydraulic systems?

Yes — tippers, crane trucks and vehicles with hydraulic tailgates are regularly serviced and repaired in our workshop. We diagnose and repair hydraulic pumps, cylinders, hoses and valve banks, and can fabricate replacement hydraulic lines.
