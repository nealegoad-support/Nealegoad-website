Do you offer free pickup and delivery?

Yes. We offer free vehicle pickup and delivery within 10 km of the Ballarat CBD. This covers Wendouree, Alfredton, Delacombe, Sebastopol, Lake Wendouree and surrounding areas. Call us to arrange collection.

What services are available at your Wendouree workshop?

Our Wendouree workshop offers the full range of automotive services: roadworthy certificates, logbook servicing, car servicing, mechanical repairs, EV and hybrid service, air conditioning, diesel and 4WD repairs, fleet servicing, and classic and vintage vehicle inspections.

How do I book a service?

Call us on (03) 5339 2056 during business hours (Mon–Fri 8am–5pm). We'll confirm your service type, arrange a time, and organise free pickup from your Ballarat address if needed.

Yes. We are a Repco Authorised Service Centre, which means all work is backed by the Repco nationwide warranty — valid at any Repco Authorised Service Centre across Australia. We are also a Licensed Vehicle Tester (LVT) with two accredited inspectors on-site.
